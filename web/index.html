How it works

  1. Watch an expert

    A scripted controller that can read the true cube position solves the task. The policy trains on 2,000 of its runs: 100,000 camera frames, each paired with the expert's action.

  2. Learn from pixels

    A ResNet-18 learns to copy the expert from the image alone, plus the gripper's own position and the target. It predicts 8 moves at once and re-plans every 4.

  3. Test on scenes it never saw

    Every number below comes from 300 held-out scenes. Each failure gets a label: missed the grasp, dropped it, wrong place, or timed out.

The demo sends the arm back to its start pose before each new job, because training always started there. If you move the cube while the arm is mid-reach, it fails more often (about 1 in 3 works in my tests). That's a known limit, not a bug in the page.
